    01 · Activity

    Know exactly who shows up.

    A Roblox module reports who (Alvin) is in your game every minute. The dashboard turns it into hours, sessions, idle time and chat activity for every member in a tracked rank.

    Total hours, daily or weekly

    Switch the graph between days and weeks, then filter the leaderboard by today, this week, last week, 30 days, all time or any custom date range.

    • A weekday-by-hour heatmap of when members actually play
    • AFK time detected server-side
    • Sessions, chat messages and who is in game right now
    • CSV export, and manual adjustments that are always logged

    A profile for every member

    Twelve weeks of history, recent play sessions, requirement results, time off, linked Discord, plus notes and warnings your staff and Alvin can leave.

    Daily & weekly requirements

    Set a minimum, override it per rank, and pick the hour checks run. Anyone who misses it is logged and posted to a Discord webhook, with your own customized message, a role mention and pings for linked members.

    • Progress bars update live through the week
    • “Run check now” for any period

    Time off that actually excuses

    Members request time off from a bot or staff add it directly. Approved time off overlapping a period automatically excuses them from a requirement check.

    02 · Ranking

    Promote without leaving the dashboard.

    Promote, demote or set any rank through Roblox Open Cloud, from a member’s profile, the ranking page, an accepted application or the API.

    • A ceiling on the highest rank the system may assign
    • The group owner can never be touched
    • Every change logged with who, why and from/to
    03 · Promotions

    Let your team pick who’s next.

    Anyone can nominate a member for promotion. Staff upvote with a short justification, so every vote comes with a reason, and a ranker can promote straight from the card.

    • Filter by rank, sort by most votes, trending or newest
    • Recommender insights: who suggests most, and how often it lands
    • Promoting moves them up one rank through Open Cloud
    04 · Logging

    Everything it does, written down.

    Promotions, demotions, missed requirements, time off, applications, sign-ins, settings changes, API keys — every action lands in a searchable log, and can be mirrored to a Discord channel per group.

    rank.promoterank.demoterequirement.not_mettimeoff.approvedapplication.submittedaccess.granted
    05 · Logbook

    Warnings that expire. A record that doesn’t.

    Log warnings, strikes, suspensions, demotions, promotions and commendations with a reason and evidence link. Warnings expire on their own, and entries are revoked, never deleted.

    • Warning ticker: active warnings per member at a glance
    • Templates for common reasons
    • Promotions and demotions can change the rank at the same time
    06 · Group management

    One dashboard, every group.

    Add as many Roblox groups as you run. Each keeps its own tracked ranks, requirements, Open Cloud key, webhooks, applications and API keys

    • Tick which ranks are tracked
    • Open Cloud keys stored encrypted
    • Sync members straight from Roblox
    07 · Applications

    Application centers you design.

    Build the questions in the dashboard. Applicants answer on the web or inside your Roblox game, and everything comes fresh from the API.

    Forms as flexible as Google Forms

    Eleven question types including date and time, an “Other” option people type into, shuffled options, scale labels and response validation (numbers, text, length, patterns or how many boxes to tick) with a customizable error message.

    Review, score, auto-rank

    See every answer with its auto-score. Your Discord server gets every answer too, with a Decide button that opens the submission. Accept and the applicant can be ranked automatically through Open Cloud.

    A shareable web form

    Every application gets a public link. Require a Discord sign-in with a linked Roblox account, or let applicants type their username.

    A Roblox place, generated

    One click downloads a ready-to-publish .rbxlx application center. It holds no questions, it fetches them from the API, so edits go live in-game instantly.

    08 · Sessions

    Session registers and targets.

    Schedule shifts, trainings and events. Staff claim them as host or co-host, and the register records who attended — which counts towards a weekly session target alongside activity minutes.

    • Paste a list of names to fill a register in one go
    • Leaderboard: most sessions this week
    • New sessions announced to Discord with a live countdown
    09 · Knowledgebase

    Handbooks, policies and news in one place.

    Write your staff handbook, guides and policies with simple formatting, grouped by category, and limit sensitive pages to people with a specific permission. Post news to the whole group and pin what matters.

    • Published at /posts and /handbooks — public, staff-only, or for one permission
    • Restricted pages ask people to sign in, then bring them straight back
    • Live preview while you write, and optional Discord announcements
    10 · Accounts

    Discord, Google and Roblox — linked.

    Members sign in with Discord, get a one-time code and type it in a verification game. The Roblox player is identified by the server, so nobody can link someone else’s account.

    • Staff sign in with Google or Discord and link the other
    • Linked members get pinged in requirement notices
    • One verification game for every group, with a Join to Verify button and an automatic kick once linked
    • Tracker module and app center downloadable per group
    11 · Access

    Sign in, pick a group, get to work.

    After signing in you choose where to work. Every group you can open shows live numbers on its card, and you can switch groups any time from the top bar.

    • Whitelist only, not on it yet? Ask for access right from the sign-in page
    • Per-person permissions: ranking, logs, applications, groups, API keys
    • Press Ctrl K to jump to any page, group or member
